\section{External validity: other benchmarks, natural retrieval, domain shift}
\label{sec:external}

We repeat the pipeline unchanged on three further sources (\cref{tab:external}): \textbf{HotpotQA} (distractor setting; 2{,}000 questions, closed-book-correct ones kept); \textbf{RAGBench}, 3{,}000 mostly single-hop questions from twelve sub-datasets across biomedical, finance, technical, customer-support and general domains, whose evidence units are the \emph{naturally retrieved} documents of the original RAG pipelines (gold: documents with annotated relevant sentences; distractors: the other retrieved documents); and a \textbf{synthetic} benchmark of 2{,}400 questions over fictional entities (closed-book accuracy zero) with one to three hops, five template families per relation, and paraphrase, partial and contradictory variants of every gold fact.

\paragraph{Readouts.}
Sufficiency is decodable on every source ($0.98$ HotpotQA, $0.90$ RAGBench, $0.97$ synthetic) and against matched controls ($0.96$, $0.94$, $0.95$), which on the synthetic data include paraphrastic redundancy, partial evidence and contradictions; the projection ordering there is decisive (chain, leave-one-out or paraphrased) $>$ contradictory $>$ partial $\approx$ redundant $>$ distractor $>$ answer-string control. Uptake is decodable on all three ($0.73$, $0.69$, $0.93$; RAGBench labels from an LLM judge against its long-form references), and the per-group breakdown (\cref{tab:groups}, \cref{app:additional}) shows it is not a multi-hop artefact: on RAGBench single-hop questions it reaches $0.70$ ($n{=}444$), on HotpotQA comparison questions, whose two facts are independent lookups, $0.79$ against $0.68$ for bridge questions, and on single-hop synthetic questions $0.99$. It is above chance in every RAGBench domain ($0.56$--$0.78$) and, on HotpotQA, in both closed-book-correct ($0.80$) and closed-book-incorrect ($0.72$) questions, while sufficiency is decoded equally in both groups ($0.984$ vs $0.978$): the readout is not a ``need for evidence'' signal.

\paragraph{Transfer across construction and domain.}
The MuSiQue directions transfer to HotpotQA at $0.95$ (sufficiency) but only partially to RAGBench ($0.71$; matched $0.78$; uptake $0.55$) and to the synthetic data ($0.74$; uptake $0.72$; correction $0.80$). Within RAGBench, holding out whole sub-datasets drops sufficiency from $0.90$ to $0.72$ (matched $0.94\to0.87$, uptake $0.69\to0.60$), and holding out relation chains on the synthetic data costs two to five points. The state is therefore present under natural retrieval and in every domain, but its direction is partly dataset-specific; a Wikipedia-trained detector should not be deployed unadapted on biomedical or technical RAG.

\paragraph{Causal dissociation.}
The dissociation replicates on all three sources: the full anchor patch makes the insufficient prompt answer ($0.41\to0.92$, $0.15\to0.72$, $0.03\to0.89$), the probe-direction component does nothing, the orthogonal component carries the effect, and projecting out $\vdm$ silences answering ($0.17$, $0.10$, $0.00$) while a random direction does not. On synthetic data the patched model answers $89\%$ of the time but is never correct: the anchor carries the decision to answer, while a fictional answer can only be read from evidence tokens the insufficient prompt lacks.

\paragraph{Evidence State Transition Benchmark: retrieval source and linguistic form.}
We rebuild MuSiQue trajectories with evidence from the 21M-passage Wikipedia corpus of FlashRAG, retrieved by BM25 or E5 with one query per hop: either the gold paragraphs are kept and all distractors are retrieved, or \emph{all} evidence is retrieved (402 questions for E5, 219 for BM25, where retrieval found every hop). A further run adds LLM-paraphrased and value-withheld (partial) versions of each gold paragraph and independently authored paragraphs on the same entity from HotpotQA or \twowiki{}. Nothing changes qualitatively (\cref{tab:estb}, \cref{app:additional}): sufficiency stays at $0.91$--$0.95$, the MuSiQue directions transfer at $0.91$--$0.94$ (sufficiency) and $0.76$--$0.82$ (uptake) on questions the source probes never saw, and the causal dissociation and abstention gate reproduce in every cell. Paraphrased and independently authored decisive paragraphs project like the originals; partial and contradictory ones are the hardest controls ($0.81$, $0.76$; \cref{tab:estb_quality}).
