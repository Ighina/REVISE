\section{Conclusion}
Paired activation updates over matched evidence trajectories give a clean experimental handle on what a language model registers when evidence arrives. They show that evidence sufficiency is readable before generation, transferable and text-recoverable; that evidence uptake is readable, transferable and \emph{not} text-recoverable; and that neither readout direction is causally responsible for the behaviour it predicts. The one causal direction we find is an abstention gate. We release the trajectory construction, probing and intervention code to make the readout-versus-mechanism distinction a standard check for future work on evidence-aware generation.
