\section{Conclusion}
We compared the model's internal state before and after each new piece of evidence, using evidence trajectories with matched controls. Evidence sufficiency is readable before generation, transfers across datasets, and can also be recovered from the text. Evidence uptake is readable and transfers, but cannot be recovered from the text. Neither readout direction causes the behaviour it predicts; the one causal direction we found is an abstention gate. We release the trajectory construction, probing and intervention code, so that checking whether a readout is also a mechanism can become standard practice in studies of evidence-aware generation.
