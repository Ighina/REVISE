\section{External validity: other benchmarks, natural retrieval, domain shift}
\label{sec:external}

The results so far come from \musique{}, a dataset whose questions were composed from single-hop questions over curated Wikipedia paragraphs. We now ask whether they depend on that construction. We repeat the whole pipeline, unchanged and with Qwen2.5-7B, on three further sources (\cref{tab:external}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{HotpotQA} \cite{yang2018hotpotqa} was crowdsourced to test multi-hop reasoning over pairs of Wikipedia paragraphs, with annotated supporting sentences so that models can be asked to explain their answers. It contains both \emph{bridge} questions, where the first paragraph leads to the entity the second one describes, and \emph{comparison} questions, whose two facts are independent lookups. We use 2{,}000 questions from its distractor setting, taking the two supporting paragraphs as gold hops and the provided distractor paragraphs as distractors. Unlike in the main runs, we keep questions the model can answer closed-book, so that results can be split by closed-book status.
  \item \textbf{RAGBench} \cite{friel2024ragbench} was built to evaluate RAG systems on realistic inputs. It collects twelve existing question-answering datasets from biomedical, finance, technical, customer-support and general domains, together with the documents a real retrieval pipeline returned for each question, and annotates which sentences of those documents support the reference answer. We use 3{,}000 of its mostly single-hop questions. The documents with annotated relevant sentences are the gold evidence and the other retrieved documents are the distractors, so here the distractors are \emph{naturally retrieved} rather than curated. RAGBench provides long-form reference answers, so we judge correctness with an LLM judge that compares the model's answer with the reference.
  \item \textbf{A synthetic benchmark}, which we generate, provides full control over the evidence. Its 2{,}400 questions concern fictional entities, so the model cannot know any answer closed-book (its closed-book accuracy is zero). The questions require one to three hops over seven relations, each expressed with five template families. Every gold fact also comes in a paraphrased, a partial (value withheld) and a contradictory (different value) version, and the distractors include near misses such as the same relation for another entity.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Readouts.}
Sufficiency is decodable on every source ($0.98$ HotpotQA, $0.90$ RAGBench, $0.97$ synthetic) and against matched controls ($0.96$, $0.94$, $0.95$). On the synthetic data, these controls include paraphrased duplicates, partial evidence and contradictions. Projected onto the sufficiency direction, the synthetic increments are ordered as on \musique{}: decisive (whether original, paraphrased or completing a leave-one-out context) $>$ contradictory $>$ partial $\approx$ duplicate $>$ distractor $>$ answer-string control. Uptake is also decodable on all three sources ($0.73$, $0.69$, $0.93$). Breaking results down by question group (\cref{tab:groups}, \cref{app:groups}) shows that uptake is not an artefact of multi-hop questions. It reaches $0.70$ on RAGBench single-hop questions ($n{=}444$), $0.79$ on HotpotQA comparison questions against $0.68$ for bridge questions, and $0.99$ on single-hop synthetic questions. It is above chance in every RAGBench domain ($0.56$--$0.78$). On HotpotQA it is above chance both for questions the model answers correctly closed-book ($0.80$) and for those it does not ($0.72$). Sufficiency is decoded equally well in both groups ($0.984$ against $0.978$), so the readout is not a signal that the model ``needs'' evidence it lacks.

\paragraph{Transfer across construction and domain.}
The \musique{} directions transfer to HotpotQA at $0.95$ for sufficiency, but only partially to RAGBench ($0.71$; matched $0.78$; uptake $0.55$) and to the synthetic data ($0.74$; uptake $0.72$; correction $0.80$). Within RAGBench, holding out whole sub-datasets at test time drops sufficiency from $0.90$ to $0.72$ (matched $0.94\to0.87$, uptake $0.69\to0.60$). Holding out relation chains on the synthetic data costs two to five points. The state is therefore present under natural retrieval and in every domain, but its direction is partly dataset-specific: a detector trained on Wikipedia should not be deployed without adaptation on biomedical or technical RAG.

\paragraph{Causal dissociation.}
The dissociation of \cref{sec:causal} replicates on all three sources. The full anchor patch makes the insufficient prompt answer ($0.41\to0.92$, $0.15\to0.72$, $0.03\to0.89$), the component along the probe direction does nothing, and the orthogonal component carries the whole effect. Projecting out $\vdm$ silences answering ($0.17$, $0.10$, $0.00$), while a random direction does not. On synthetic data the patched model answers $89\%$ of the time but is never correct. The anchor carries the decision to answer, while a fictional answer can only be read from the evidence tokens, which the insufficient prompt lacks.

\paragraph{Evidence State Transition Benchmark: retrieval source and linguistic form.}
Finally, we vary how the evidence itself is obtained and worded, while keeping the \musique{} questions. In a real RAG system, evidence comes from a retriever rather than from a benchmark's curated paragraphs. We therefore rebuild the trajectories of 1{,}200 \musique{} questions with passages retrieved from the 21M-passage Wikipedia corpus distributed with FlashRAG \cite{jin2025flashrag,karpukhin2020dense}, using either BM25 \cite{robertson2009bm25}, a lexical retriever, or E5 \cite{wang2022e5}, a dense one. Each question issues the question itself plus one query per hop. In one setting the gold paragraphs are kept and all distractors are retrieved. In the other, \emph{all} evidence is retrieved, which is possible for the 402 (E5) and 219 (BM25) questions for which retrieval found every hop. A further run varies the wording of the decisive paragraph: it adds LLM-paraphrased and value-withheld (partial) versions of each gold paragraph, and paragraphs on the same entity written independently for HotpotQA or \twowiki{}. Nothing changes qualitatively (\cref{tab:estb}; construction, measurements and full results in \cref{app:estb}). Sufficiency stays at $0.91$--$0.95$. The \musique{} directions transfer at $0.91$--$0.94$ (sufficiency) and $0.76$--$0.82$ (uptake) on questions the source probes never saw, and the causal dissociation and abstention gate reproduce in every setting. Paraphrased and independently written decisive paragraphs project like the originals. Partial and contradictory versions are the hardest controls, separated from decisive increments at only $0.81$ and $0.76$ (\cref{tab:estb_quality}).
