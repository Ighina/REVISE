\section{Conclusion}
Paired activation updates over matched evidence trajectories show that evidence sufficiency is readable before generation, transferable and text-recoverable; that evidence uptake is readable, transferable and \emph{not} text-recoverable; and that neither readout direction causes the behaviour it predicts, the one causal direction being an abstention gate. We release the trajectory construction, probing and intervention code so that the readout-versus-mechanism check becomes standard for evidence-aware generation.
